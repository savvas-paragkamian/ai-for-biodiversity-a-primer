Thank you for your interest in the workshop. It is a one-day course based on
the primer *AI for Biodiversity* and its hands-on companion. The programme
covers the landscape of language models and agents, AI-ready biodiversity
data, working with coding agents, running models on your own machine
(local-first), and risk, integrity and keeping up. The hands-on sessions are
in R and use Git, a terminal, Ollama and a coding harness (OpenCode, Claude
Code or Codex).

This form takes about five minutes. Your answers help us set the level of
the programming parts, choose which tools to show, and collect the questions
you want explored. Answers are seen only by the organisers and are used only
to prepare the workshop. Fields marked * are required.

Legend for building the form in Google Forms or Microsoft Forms:
*dropdown* = single choice from a list · *checkboxes* = choose all that
apply · *scale* = linear scale · *short* = one line of text ·
*paragraph* = free text.
