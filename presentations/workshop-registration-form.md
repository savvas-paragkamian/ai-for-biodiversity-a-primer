Thank you for your interest. The workshop is a one-day course taught from
the book *AI for Biodiversity: A Primer* and its hands-on companion. The
morning has two lecture sessions: the landscape and the data, then working
with agents and running models on your own machine. After lunch comes a
short setup session, then the hands-on session in R. It has three parts: a
first agent session on an invented springtail survey, a weekly literature
review run offline, and a test of a model against an answer key. The day
closes with security,
integrity and keeping up, and a discussion on what to tell colleagues. The
case that ecology is not ready for AI is taught alongside the rest.

This form takes about five minutes; the optional questions add two more.
Your answers set the level of the programming parts, decide which tools we
show, and collect the questions you want explored. Only the organisers see
them, and they are reported only in aggregate. Fields marked * are required.

Legend for building the form in Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or LimeSurvey.
Each numbered item is one question; the word in italics is its type:
*dropdown* = one choice from a list · *checkboxes* = all that apply ·
*scale* = linear scale · *short* = one line of text · *paragraph* = free
text. "Other (please specify)" is the tool's own "Other" option.
