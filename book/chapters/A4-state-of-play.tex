\chapter{State of Play, September 2026}
\label{app:stateofplay}

The names, prices and limits below record the situation in September 2026.
They will change, so the chapters explain the reasoning behind these choices
and point here for the details available at the time of writing.

\textbf{Under 2,000 euros}, a 48\,GB Mac mini or a 64\,GB AMD mini-PC runs a
30B model usefully: the entry point for serious local work. \textbf{Around
3,000}, a 128\,GB Mac Studio or DGX Spark opens 70B models, where open weights
become competitive for scoped work; for a group, one such machine can serve
several people. \textbf{A separate graphics card} is the choice if you also need
to train, or want maximum speed on models under 32\,GB. Its 600\,W affects
cooling, the electricity bill and where the machine can live.

\section{Models}

Coding and general models of 12--30B: the Gemma 4, Kimi K2.7 and Qwen 3.7--3.8
families. Embedding: \code{bge-m3}; reranking: \code{bge-reranker}.
Transcription: Whisper.

\section{Harnesses}

In the terminal: Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Gemini CLI, Copilot CLI and
Aider. Inside code editors: Cline and Continue. \Cref{app:tutorials} links to
the open-source ones.

\section{Subscription limits}

Consumer subscription tiers ration use by a rolling five-hour window, on the
order of tens of thousands of tokens per window.
